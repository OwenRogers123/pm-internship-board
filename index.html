Standing notes

Positioning

Business + Information Systems + a real sales job is an edge for enterprise SaaS PM, where teams want someone who has actually talked to customers. For outdoor and lifestyle brands, genuine product passion carries unusual weight — small applicant pools, hiring for enthusiasm plus judgment.

How this stays current

Built because manually re-checking 40+ career pages every week for one eligibility line ("pursuing a degree in CS, Engineering, or a related field") is exactly the kind of chore that eats a Tuesday night. An AI agent runs the sweep instead: GitHub tracker → LinkedIn/Google search → company boards → re-verify everything already on the board — then writes the results here and to a version-controlled markdown archive, so a role that was already ruled out (closed, MBA-only, wrong degree bar) never wastes a re-check.
