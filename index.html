How I get my picks

Search can't tell you if a role quietly requires a CS degree. That's in the fine print, and checking 40+ career pages for it every week takes hours. So an AI agent reads it for me.

1

Scan

Every two days it searches the web: job boards, LinkedIn, Google, and about 40 California company career pages.

2

Filter

It opens each posting and reads the actual requirements. A role only makes the list if it passes all three rules below.

3

Update

It rewrites this page, emails me the results every 2 days, and keeps a log of past mistakes so a closed or wrong role never shows up twice.
